# Main-results audit and Stage M continuation

Status: prospective development-only continuation after the consumed Stage-A v3-2 negative.

## Evidence-preserving conclusion

Stage-A v3-2 remains terminal for its exact registration. Its all-token model-local
compiler did not qualify the one-shot directed-edge packet. Semantic Brier,
unseen-renderer transfer, activation specificity, and conjunctive composition
failed, while behavioral prediction, public alignment, held-sender retention,
quantization, and the exact oracle executor passed.

The result must not be retried or tuned. It also must not be broadened into the
claim that no public operational state exists.

## Missing mathematical distinction

The public edge packet stores first-order marginal probabilities. The frozen
executor then treats uncertain edges as independent. For nonlinear operations,
that is not generally sufficient:

```text
E[f(E)] != f(E[E]).
```

Mutuality needs a reciprocal-edge conjunction probability. Two-hop reachability
needs path conjunctions and their intersections. Out-degree comparison needs a
joint degree distribution. A failed nonlinear composition gate can therefore
mean either that information is absent or that the public basis is not closed
under the registered operation algebra.

## Stage M question

Stage M0 asks whether frozen sources can expose the joint public events needed to
close the four-entity operation grammar, and whether an exact affine executor over
those events beats both:

1. the historical first-order marginal/independence executor; and
2. a direct response protocol selected on a disjoint development role.

This is interactive event tomography. It is not a one-shot latent interface,
held-sender result, receiver result, or paper claim.

## Why this is the next admissible experiment

The previous line already established that atomic public facts are easier to
recover and compose than direct answers under RC0, while the one-shot compiler
failed on semantic calibration and nonlinear strata. Stage M isolates whether
that gap comes from source information or from an undercomplete public state.

A negative event-readout result stops the current graph/source line. A positive
result justifies one separately registered model-local compiler into the same
operation-closed coordinates. The final ICLR paper still requires a task where
the sufficient state must be computed by filtering or closure rather than copied
from explicit graph text.
