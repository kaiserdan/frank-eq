- the stronger-checkpoint screens remain valid negatives for the exact
  immediate answer-token protocol;

Correction:

- the Stage-Q screens did not isolate source-state sufficiency;
- they measured an immediate A/B answer probability with zero additional
  autoregressive scratchpad tokens;
- source Brier therefore combined answer-channel calibration, post-query
  computation, and state information.

Reason:

- at Qwen3-8B, single-edge/global families begin passing while multi-edge
  composition remains strongly negative;
- this pattern is compatible with missing state, but also with a readout/compute
  bottleneck;
- continued scale screening under the same response contract cannot distinguish
  those explanations.
